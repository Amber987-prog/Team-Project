What is Multiplication?

Multiplication is a fundamental arithmetic operation representing repeated addition, where a number (the multiplicand) is added to itself a specific number of times (the multiplier).

Key Terminology

  • Factor - The individual numbers being multiplied together.
  • Product - The final result or answer of the multiplication.
  • Multiplier - The number by which you multiply, often indicating the number of groups.
  • Multiple - A number that can be divided by another number without a remainder

Worked Examples

Example 1: 1: 4 x 6 = 24

Example 2: 34 x 5 = 70

Example 3: 10 x 50 = 500
