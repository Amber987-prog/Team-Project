What is Addition?

Addition is the process of combining two or more numbers together to find the total. It is one of the four fundamental operations in arithmetic.

Key Terminology

  • Addend – a number that is being added
  • Sum – the final result of an addition
  • Plus (+) – the symbol used to represent addition
  • Fundamental operation – one of the basic arithmetic operations (addition, subtraction, multiplication, division)

Worked Examples

Example 1: 15 + 5 = 20

Example 2: 144 + 12 = 156

Example 3: 21 + 5 = 26
