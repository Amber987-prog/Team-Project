What is Division?

Division is a method of splitting a total amount into smaller equal parts. It is represented using the ÷ symbol or the forward slash /. Division is the inverse of multiplication and is used to show sharing or grouping. Some division calculations include a remainder when the number cannot be split equally.

Key Terminology

  • Dividend – the number being divided
  • Divisor – the number you are dividing by
  • Quotient – the final answer
  • Remainder – the amount left over after division

Worked Examples

Example 1: 15 ÷ 5 = 3

Example 2: 144 ÷ 12 = 12

Example 3: 21 ÷ 5 = 4 remainder 1
