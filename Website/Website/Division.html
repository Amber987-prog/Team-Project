Division

What is Division?

Division is a method of splitting a total amount into smaller or equal parts. It is represented with the ÷ symbol or the forward slash /. Division is the inverse of multiplication, and portrays the grouping/sharing of numbers, which can often result in a remainder if the calculation is not split up equally.

Key Terminology

  • Dividend – the specific number that is being divided
  • Divisor – the specific number that is dividing by
  • Quotient – the final answer
  • Remainder – what is remaining after division

Worked examples

Example 1: 15 ÷ 5 = 3

Example 2: 144 ÷ 12 = 12

Example 3: 21 ÷ 5 = 4 remainder 1
