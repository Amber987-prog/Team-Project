Addition

Learn how to add complex numbers together, then take the quiz to earn Bronze, Silver or Gold.

What is Addition?

Addition is the process of calculating two or more numbers together

Key Terminology

  • Addend – the specific number that is being added
  • Sum – the final answer
  • Plus – the symbol used to represent addition (+)
  • Fundamental operation – one of the basic operations in arithmetic (addition, subtraction, multiplication, division)

Worked examples

Example 1: 15 + 5 = 20

Example 2: 144 + 12 = 156

Example 3: 21 + 5 = 26
