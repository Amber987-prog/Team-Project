Subtraction

Learn the theory on how to subtract numbers. Then test you knowledge by taking the quiz to earn Bronze, Silver or Gold.

What is Subtraction?

Subtraction is the process or skill of taking one number or amount away from another.

Key Terminology

  • Difference - The result of the subtraction operation (minuend - subtrahend = difference).
  • Minuend - The initial number or the total amount from which a value is subtracted.
  • Subtrahend - The amount being taken away or subtracted from the minuend.
  • Column Subtraction - A vertical method for calculating, arranging numbers by place value.

Worked Examples

Example 1: - 9 = 5

Example 2: 56 - 17 = 39

Example 3:457 - 163 = 294
